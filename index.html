Blog and Resources page

Why?

I'm visiting Paris for the first time. I want to see how much French I can learn in a month while working full-time, trip planning, and going on unrelated travel for 10 of those days.

The general plan

This is for fun, but I'm under no illusions about being conversant in a few weeks with at most 30 min a day of some kind of practice. I picked up Fluent Forever by Gabriel Wyner at the library and will follow some of his recommendations for learning a foreign language. I'll list the resources I use as the month progresses.

Background disclaimer

I took two years of high school French years ago, am solidly beginning A1, and I probably know the meaning of a maximum of 50 French words. Clearly my French classes didn't stick very well.

  1. Focus first on mastering pronunciation
  2. 1 hour: listen to French podcasts during weekday commutes
  3. 30 min each weekday: build Anki vocabulary deck and practice recalling vocabulary
  4. A couple hours on the weekends to practice vocabulary, grammar rules, and watching French TV.
