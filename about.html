One Month to Bonjour

I had a trip planned to Paris, France in July 2026 and my goal was to study French for 30 minutes a day for a few weeks prior to my trip. At the same time, I was working on the frontend development Odin Project course. Hence, this little website One Month to Bonjour was born.

Longterm, I'm interested in studying French, impoving my German skills from A2/B1 to B2, and starting to study Mandarin. So, this project will not be limited to French, but French was the inspiration to add structure to my language studies. My motivation for learning languages is varied: it's a way to connect with people I know, people I love, and strangers alike, better understand cultures other than my own, be able to read and watch untranslated books and media in multiple languages, write in multiple languages, the opportunity to be a nerd about pronunciation, tones, the visual and phonetic roots of characters in Mandarin, and other things that interest me.

With that said, whether you are going to France or another French-speaking country, like learning languages, or none of the above and are just curious, welcome to my little Internet corner of language learning.
